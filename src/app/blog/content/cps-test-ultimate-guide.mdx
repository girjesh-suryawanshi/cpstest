A **CPS test** is a simple yet powerful online tool that measures how many times you can click your mouse button within a specific timeframe, usually between one and ten seconds. ## How to Increase CPS Speed: From Beginner to Pro

Ready to boost your score? Improving your clicking speed is a marathon, not a sprint. It involves a combination of the right technique, consistent practice, and proper equipment.

### H3: Foundational Techniques for a Better Click Speed Test Score

1.  **Relax Your Hand and Arm:** Tension is your enemy. A stiff arm and wrist will slow you down and can lead to strain. Keep your hand, wrist, and forearm relaxed. Your fingers should do most of the work.
2.  **Find the Right Grip:** There's no single "correct" way to hold a mouse. Experiment with different grips (palm, claw, or fingertip) to find what feels most comfortable and allows for rapid, controlled clicks.
3.  **Focus on Consistency:** Don't try to slam the button as hard as you can. A light, consistent tap is much more effective and sustainable over the duration of a **tapping speed test**.

### Advanced Clicking Methods: Jitter and Butterfly Clicking

Once you've mastered the basics, you can explore advanced techniques. These methods are popular among top gamers and can dramatically increase your CPS.

*   **Jitter Clicking:** This technique involves tensing your arm to create rapid vibrations in your hand and finger, causing you to click the mouse button incredibly fast. While effective, the **jitter click test** can be strenuous, so it's crucial to take frequent breaks to avoid injury.
*   **Butterfly Clicking:** The butterfly method involves using two fingers (usually your index and middle finger) to alternately click the mouse button. This can double your clicking speed but requires a mouse that can register clicks without a "debounce delay." Our **jitter and butterfly clicking test** is perfect for practicing both of these advanced methods.
