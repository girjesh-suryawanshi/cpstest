## Advanced Clicking Techniques: Jitter vs. Butterfly

Ready to take your skills to the next level? Standard clicking will only get you so far. To achieve truly high CPS scores, you need to master advanced techniques.

### The Jitter Click Test

Jitter clicking involves tensing your arm and hand muscles to create rapid vibrations, which you then channel into the mouse button. It's an intense technique that can significantly boost your CPS. Our **jitter click test** is specifically designed to help you practice and perfect this method.

**How to Jitter Click:**
- Tense your wrist and forearm.
- Let the vibrations from your arm do the clicking.
- Use one finger, usually your index finger.
- Be mindful of strain; take frequent breaks.

### The Butterfly and Kohi Click Test

Butterfly clicking involves using two fingers (usually the index and middle finger) to alternately click the mouse button. This can produce even higher CPS than jittering, but it requires more coordination and may be considered cheating in some gaming communities.

The **Kohi click test** is a popular benchmark in the Minecraft community, typically lasting 10 seconds. It’s the perfect arena to practice your butterfly or jitter techniques. Our **jitter and butterfly clicking test** provides the ideal environment to hone these skills.
