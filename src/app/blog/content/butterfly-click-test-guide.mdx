## What Exactly is Butterfly Clicking?
 
Butterfly clicking is an advanced mouse technique where a player uses two fingers—typically the index and middle fingers—to rapidly alternate clicks on a single mouse button. The motion resembles the gentle, quick flapping of a butterfly's wings, hence the name. Unlike a standard single-finger click, this method can dramatically **increase CPS speed**, often allowing players to reach 20 clicks per second or even higher.
 
It’s a popular method alongside the **jitter click test**, but many find it more controlled and less strenuous on the arm. If you’re serious about gaming, particularly for titles like Minecraft, learning this skill is a game-changer. Our **click test online** is the perfect training ground.

## Pro Tips to Increase Your CPS Speed
 
Getting a high score isn't just about raw speed; it's about technique. Here are some expert tips to help you master butterfly clicking.
 
### Find the Right Grip
Experiment with how you hold your mouse. Some players find a palm grip more stable, while others prefer a claw grip for faster finger action. There's no one-size-fits-all answer, so find what works for you.
 
### Use a Mouse That Supports It
Not all mice are created equal. Some gaming mice have sensitive switches that are better suited for butterfly and drag clicking. A mouse that can register "double clicks" from a single press can also artificially inflate your CPS, which is something many **CPS test for Minecraft players** look for.
 
### Practice in Short Bursts
Don't overdo it. Practice in short intervals, like our 20-second test or even a **1 second click test online**, to build muscle memory without causing strain. Consistency is more important than long, grueling sessions.
 
### Stay Relaxed
Tensing your entire arm will lead to fatigue and slower clicks. The motion should come from your fingers and hand, not your shoulder. Stay relaxed and focus on a smooth, controlled rhythm. Our **tapping speed game for mobile** and desktop is designed to be a low-stress training tool.
