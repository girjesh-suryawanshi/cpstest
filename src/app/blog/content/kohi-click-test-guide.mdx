## Advanced Techniques: How to Increase CPS Speed

Achieving a high score on a **click test online** isn’t just about raw strength; it’s about technique. Here are the most popular methods used by pros to dominate the **jitter and butterfly clicking test**.

### Jitter Clicking

The **jitter click test** technique involves tensing your arm and wrist to create rapid vibrations, causing your finger to tap the mouse button at an incredible speed.

-   **How to Do It:** Rest your finger lightly on the mouse button. Tense your forearm and wrist muscles until your hand begins to shake or "jitter." Channel this vibration into your clicking finger.
-   **Pros:** Can produce very high CPS (10-15).
-   **Cons:** It's tiring and can cause strain if done for too long. It also makes aiming more difficult.

### Butterfly Clicking

This method involves using two fingers (usually your index and middle finger) to alternately click the same mouse button.

-   **How to Do It:** Place your index and middle fingers on the left mouse button. Rapidly alternate tapping them, as if you're drumming your fingers on a table.
-   **Pros:** Can achieve the highest CPS (15-25+). Less strenuous than jitter clicking over short periods.
-   **Cons:** Requires a mouse that can register clicks this fast without double-clicking issues. Not all mice are suitable.

### Other Clicking Methods

-   **Regular Clicking:** The standard method. Good for accuracy, but CPS is typically limited to 4-8.
-   **Drag Clicking:** An advanced and risky method where you drag your finger across the button to register dozens of clicks. This can damage your mouse and is often banned on servers.
